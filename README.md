## About Me

I’m a Junior Gameplay Programmer specialized in **Unity and C#**, with experience developing gameplay systems, multiplayer features, user interfaces, tools, and virtual reality applications.

I enjoy building complete and scalable systems, from player controllers and combat mechanics to networking, procedural gameplay, inventories, loot systems, and technical tools.

I trained at **Créajeux** through several team-based game projects and gained professional experience at **Streetlab**, where I worked on a Unity VR application for Meta Quest.

## Professional Experience

### Unity VR Developer — Streetlab

At Streetlab, I worked on a **Unity VR runner application for Meta Quest**, designed for mobility and perception experiments.

My work included headset-based lane detection, procedural environment movement, obstacle generation, collision evaluation, runtime configuration, and communication between the VR headset and a PC monitoring application.

`Unity` `C#` `Meta Quest` `VR` `WebSocket` `Gameplay Tools`

## Featured Projects

### Project Survivor — Solo Project

An online hack-and-slash inspired by games such as *Path of Exile* and *Vampire Survivors*, developed independently with **Unity, C#, and Mirror**.

The project includes online multiplayer, a town hub, instanced maps, character statistics, abilities, enemies, loot generation, equipment, inventories, trading, and difficulty scaling.

It also uses a dedicated Linux server and an automated Jenkins build pipeline.

[View the repository](https://github.com/Im0-R/Project-Survivor)

`Unity` `C#` `Mirror` `Multiplayer` `Game Architecture` `Linux Server`

### Vorace — Team of 8

A roguelite in which you play as a pirate who has lost everything and sets out to reclaim what once belonged to him.

This released school project was developed by a multidisciplinary team of eight students.

`Unity` `C#` `Roguelite` `Team Project`

### Project Time Capsule — Team of 8

A narrative puzzle game in which you explore your childhood home from both an adult and childhood perspective.

Players solve environmental puzzles while uncovering memories and searching for a way to escape their solitude.

`Unity` `C#` `Puzzle Game` `Narrative` `Team Project`

### The Fallen One — Team of 8

A boss-fight game developed in **C++ with SFML** as part of a multidisciplinary school team.

[View the repository](https://github.com/Im0-R/TheFallenOne)

`C++` `SFML` `Boss Fight` `Gameplay Programming`
